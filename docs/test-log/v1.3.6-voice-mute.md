# v1.3.6: mute the microphone while a reply is spoken (PR pending)

**Status**: NOT RUN
**Needs**: a microphone, laptop speakers, AI Assistant and Voice switched on, a working AI provider key; for check 7 also AI speech set up with a provider key
**Time**: 25 minutes

The automated tests cover the setting, the conversation states and the timing. These checks need a real microphone and speakers.

## The setting

1. **The checkbox in Settings**
   Do: Open Settings > Voice. Set Mode to Conversation. Look for the checkbox. Then set Mode to Dictation.
   Expect: With Conversation, "Mute the microphone while a reply is spoken" is there, unticked and enabled. With Dictation it is greyed.
   If it fails: Note the Mode and what the checkbox shows.
   Result:

2. **It is saved**
   Do: Set Mode to Conversation, tick "Mute the microphone while a reply is spoken" and click Save. Close Nutshell and start it again. Open Settings > Voice.
   Expect: The checkbox is still ticked. No notice appears at launch.
   If it fails: Note whether it was cleared or a notice appeared, and its text.
   Result:

## While a reply is spoken

3. **The header while muted**
   Do: With the box ticked, start a conversation and ask something that gets a long answer. Watch the voice header while the reply plays.
   Expect: The header reads "Microphone off while the assistant speaks. Press the voice key to interrupt."
   If it fails: Note what the header said.
   Result:

4. **Speaking over the reply**
   Do: On laptop speakers at high volume, talk or clap over the reply.
   Expect: The reply neither dips in volume nor stops.
   If it fails: Note whether it dipped or stopped, and the volume.
   Result:

5. **The reply ends**
   Do: Let a reply finish. Wait a moment, then say a sentence.
   Expect: About a third of a second after the reply the header returns to its listening text. Your sentence starts a turn normally and none of the assistant's words are in it.
   If it fails: Note the header and what the turn contains.
   Result:

6. **Interrupting with a key**
   Do: Press Right Shift (or your voice key) in the middle of a reply. Repeat with the Stop button, then with Escape in the panel.
   Expect: Each time the speech stops at once and the assistant listens.
   If it fails: Note which of the three did not stop the speech.
   Result:

7. **AI speech**
   Do: In Settings > Voice set Speech to AI speech, then repeat checks 3 to 6.
   Expect: The results are the same as with Windows speech.
   If it fails: Note the check number and what differed.
   Result:

8. **Box unticked**
   Do: Untick "Mute the microphone while a reply is spoken", Save, and repeat check 4.
   Expect: Speaking over a reply cuts in, as before this version.
   If it fails: Note whether the reply was cut in or not.
   Result:

9. **A config from before this version**
   Do: Close Nutshell. Copy `nutshell.config` aside, then remove the line `voice_conv_mute_speaking` from the original. Start Nutshell.
   Expect: One notice names "Voice: mute the microphone while a reply is spoken: Off". The setting is now in the config file. Put your own config back afterwards.
   If it fails: Note the notice text, or that none appeared.
   Result:

## Known limits

10. **Limits you can live with**
   Do: With the box ticked, say something in the middle of a reply and again in the third of a second after it ends. Then press the voice key just after a reply ends.
   Expect: What you say during a reply, or just after it, is not heard. The key press just after the end stops the conversation instead of interrupting. Decide whether you can live with both.
   If it fails: Note which of the two bothers you and why.
